9 Authentic Latin Ingredients You'll Taste in Buena Vista's Dishes

From malanga to papaya-juice marinades — the nine ingredients that carry the heritage behind our plates.

At Buena Vista, we've always believed that the heart of Latin cuisine lies in the ingredients themselves. Before any technique, plating idea, or recipe even begins, it's the ingredients that shape the soul of a dish. When guests dine with us, we want them to taste more than flavor; we want them to taste heritage, and the kind of warmth that comes naturally from Latin cooking. That's why we choose ingredients with intention, relying on what has defined our best latin american food in NYC culture for generations and pairing it with the experience and creativity of our kitchen team.

Over time, many of our guests have asked what makes the dishes here feel so distinct. Is it the seasonings? The techniques? The combinations? The answer is simpler than most people expect: it starts with the ingredients. So in this blog, we're highlighting 9 authentic Latin ingredients you'll find in some of our signature dishes. These ingredients aren't chosen at random; they're selected because they carry history, personality, and the kind of bold yet comforting character that we want every Buena Vista plate to have.

Here's what you'll discover:

  • The ingredients we rely on
  • Why they matter in Latin food culture
  • The dishes where they shine
  • How we bring tradition into modern cooking
  • And why this list is only a preview of what we serve

What Sets Our Flavor Apart?

When we build a dish, we're not just chasing flavor; we're trying to create a moment. The kind that stays with you long after the meal. To do that, we rely on ingredients that have depth, warmth, and familiarity. Some come from long-standing Latin traditions, and others bring out the best in our proteins or sides. But together, they form the backbone of the menu our guests come back for.

A quick look at how we approach flavor:

  • We start with ingredients that matter in our culture
  • We combine comfort and refinement on the same plate
  • We focus on balance rather than heavy seasoning
  • We let the ingredients speak for themselves
  • We honor tradition while giving dishes a modern touch

With that foundation, let's look at the nine ingredients that shape some of our signature plates.

9 Authentic Latin Ingredients Featured in Our Dishes

1. Bacon-Stuffed Turkey Breast

Turkey is a familiar presence in many Latin households, especially during celebrations. We honor that by preparing our Turkey Breast stuffed with bacon, giving the dish a smoky, hearty richness that complements the tenderness of the meat. It's straightforward, satisfying, and rooted in comfort.

2. Grilled Lamb Chops with Granada

The use of granada, pomegranate, brings brightness and depth that works beautifully with grilled meats. Our Lamb Chops are balanced with this ingredient to create a contrast of savory richness and vibrant, sweet-tart flavor that many of our guests find unforgettable.

3. Oven-Roasted Spicy Potato with Artisanal Magic Jerez

Spicy potatoes are a staple in many Latin kitchens, and here they anchor our Seared Baby Calamari. The oven-roasted spicy potato with artisanal magic Jerez adds warmth, texture, and just enough heat to create a layered and satisfying bite.

4. Yogurt, Spinach, and Cilantro Foam

Herbs like cilantro have always been essential in Latin cooking, and dairy-based marinades appear across the region as well. Our Farm Raised Chicken Turnovers with yogurt, spinach, and cilantro foam bring those familiar flavors into a modern preparation that is both fresh and comforting.

5. Porchine Mushroom with Grey Goose Tomato Vodka Sauce

Our Lobster Ravioli takes on its unique personality from porchine mushrooms in a Grey Goose tomato vodka sauce, giving the dish richness, depth, and a savory note that fits naturally into Latin-style sauces and hearty entrées.

6. Pumpkin Purée Quenelles

Pumpkin appears in many Latin dishes, from soups to purées, and its smooth sweetness pairs beautifully with seafood. Our Seared Filet of Chilean Sea Bass served over pumpkin purée quenelles offers that warm, comforting balance that makes this dish one of our most elegant plates.

7. Chef's Daily Farm Market Selection

Seasonality is a big part of Latin cooking, and our Soup of the Day reflects that tradition. The dish features the chef's farm market selection daily, giving our guests a fresh and authentic tasting experience based on what's available and flavorful at the moment.

8. Spinach Over Malanga Purée in Lemongrass Infusion

Malanga is a beloved root vegetable across the Caribbean and Latin America, known for its earthy, comforting flavor. Paired with spinach and a bright lemongrass infusion, our Filet of Salmon gets a balance of creamy and fragrant elements that showcase this ingredient beautifully.

9. Papaya Juice Marinade

Papaya juice is a classic Latin method for naturally tenderizing meats. Our Angus Rib Eye steak, tenderized with papaya juice, takes on a gentle sweetness and deeper flavor, allowing the seasoning to penetrate the steak and giving the dish its signature tenderness.

Why These Ingredients Matter to Us?

For us, these ingredients aren't trends; they're part of the culinary heritage we grew up around and still draw inspiration from. They connect us to our roots, and we use them because they feel honest and personal, not because they're fashionable.

Ingredients matter to us because they:

  • Hold cultural meaning
  • Reflect on different regions of Latin America
  • Pair naturally with the proteins we choose
  • Allow us to bring tradition into modern cooking
  • Help us tell a story through our dishes

These ingredients are a small reminder of the history we bring into the kitchen every day.

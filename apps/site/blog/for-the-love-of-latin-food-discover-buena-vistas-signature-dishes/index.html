For the Love of Latin Food: Discover Buena Vista's Signature Dishes

Five signature plates that carry the heart of Latin cooking — familiar, expressive, and made with intention.

There is a certain kind of comfort that comes from Latin cooking. It is warm, colorful, layered, and shaped by generations of people who treat food as a form of connection. At Buena Vista, we try to hold onto that feeling every time we create a dish. We want guests to feel the spirit behind the food as much as the flavor itself. That means paying attention to details, listening to tradition, and bringing forward the kind of energy that makes a meal feel like an experience rather than a simple stop for dinner.

When people dine with us for the first time, they usually comment on the atmosphere before anything else. The glow of the space, the rhythm of the room, and the ease with which conversations start. But once the food arrives, the focus shifts. The dishes carry familiar elements, but they come together in a way that feels intentional and expressive, reflecting our passion for sharing the best Latin American food NYC has to offer. It is the part of the evening that stays with people, and it is the reason we decided to highlight some of the plates that best represent who we are.

How do We Think About Food at Buena Vista?

Before diving into the signature dishes, it helps to understand how we approach cooking. Latin food has a wide personality. It can be bold, subtle, bright, earthy, rich, or refreshing. The challenge is choosing how to bring those qualities forward without losing the honesty of the ingredients.

What Guides Our Kitchen

We focus on a few core ideas when we develop or refine dishes:

  • Let the ingredients speak for themselves.
  • Treat tradition with respect, not restriction.
  • Balance richness with moments of brightness.
  • Build flavors intentionally rather than relying on intensity alone.
  • Present each dish in a way that reflects care, not decoration.

These points shape how we cook, taste, adjust, and finalize every plate.

Buena Vista's Signature Dishes

These five plates carry the heart of what we love about Latin food. They show the contrast between richness and freshness, the influence of tradition, and the creativity that comes from thoughtful technique.

Mexican guacamole

Our Mexican guacamole takes a familiar classic and gives it more depth. It is served with chilaquiles and green plantain confit inside a molcajete shell. The creaminess of the avocado works well with the crunch of the chilaquiles, while the plantain confit adds a warm and slightly sweet note. The combination creates a balanced, comforting start to the meal.

Oven-roasted oxtail

This dish holds a richness that diners appreciate instantly. The oxtail is roasted until tender and finished with a black beer ajus that deepens the flavor without overpowering it. A light scatter of lima beans adds texture and a subtle earthiness. The result is a plate that feels traditional in spirit but refined in execution.

Calamari ink linguini

The calamari ink linguini brings together seafood, herbs, and a silky sauce that ties everything together. The linguini is sautéed with scallop, shrimp, and spinach in a lemongrass Alfredo that feels both creamy and bright. A touch of pecorino and micro purple basil lifts the dish with aromatics and a hint of sharpness. It is one of the most expressive plates we offer.

Filet of salmon

This salmon dish blends freshness with softness in a way that appeals to many diners. The filet is filled with spinach and set over a malanga puree that carries a gentle sweetness. The lemongrass infusion adds light citrus notes, while the cilantro foam brings an aromatic finish. It is a dish that feels rounded and balanced.

Green avocado salad

The green avocado salad offers a refreshing contrast to the richer plates. It includes organic lettuce, a red onion remoulade, and julienned asparagus. A tropical citric foam completes the dish with brightness. It is crisp and lively, making it a favorite for guests who appreciate fresh, layered salads.

Why These Plates Stand Out?

We chose these dishes as signatures because they carry the personality of our kitchen. Each one blends inspiration from Latin traditions with our own perspective on flavor and presentation. They are expressive without being complicated and comforting without being predictable.

What Guests Often Notice

Many diners share that they appreciate how these dishes balance three key qualities:

  • Familiarity
  • Creativity
  • Depth of flavor

We never want food to feel risky for the sake of being different. Instead, our goal is to bring new life to ingredients and ideas that already hold meaning in Latin cooking.

The Craft Behind Buena Vista's Menu

A lot of people ask how we keep the flavors consistent while still allowing room for creativity. The answer lies in the way we treat the process. Cooking for us is not about rushing. It is about practice, tasting, patience, and refinement. Every element on the plate has a reason for being there.

What We Pay Attention To

  • The texture of each component
  • The balance between richness and acidity
  • How layers of flavor unfold
  • The visual harmony of the dish
  • The way the dish interacts with accompanying cocktails or wines

When all of these pieces come together, the dish feels cohesive and satisfying.

The Dining Experience Beyond the Plate

The food is the center of what we do, but the environment around it matters just as much. Dining at Buena Vista is meant to feel warm and relaxed. We want people to settle in, enjoy the moment, and let the experience unfold naturally. Latin cuisine has always been tied to community and sharing, and we try to carry that feeling through every part of the evening.

Guests often tell us that Buena Vista feels like a place where they can truly unwind. Whether they are here for a quiet dinner or a lively gathering, the atmosphere creates room for conversation, connection, and enjoyment.

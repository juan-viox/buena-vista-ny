BUENA VISTA RESTAURANT & BAR — KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT
Buena Vista Restaurant & Bar is an elevated dining and cocktail destination in New York City where Spanish elegance meets Latin American soul ("Spain Meets Latin America"). Founded in 2018 by owner Christian Nuñez. Modern Latin / Spanish fine dining, price range $$$. The mission: first-class hospitality and a memorable dining experience — exceptional service, delicious food, exotic cocktails, and a carefully curated wine list.

Section 05

Hero Animation Concept

"La Paella" — a scroll-driven film. The single most important upgrade: replacing storefront footage with the dish that built the reputation.

How It Works

We generate a cinematic AI food film — slow dolly across the paella pan mid-flame, saffron steam curling in shallow depth of field, a broth pour catching candlelight, glassware and the dining room resolving in the final beat — graded dark and warm over the navy base. The film is extracted into a frame sequence and rendered through a canvas frame engine: as the guest scrolls, frames scrub in perfect sync. The guest controls the camera. Scroll slowly and the steam hangs; scroll on and the flame leaps. Type and CTAs float above on a 0.45–0.65 navy overlay for flawless readability.

Beat 1 · The Flame

Overhead dolly across the paella pan, socarrat sizzling, chorizo and saffron rice in macro.

Beat 2 · The Steam

Saffron steam rises in slow motion; "Spain Meets Latin America" sets in Libre Baskerville.

Beat 3 · The Pour

Broth pour and a sangria catchlight; the gold Reserve CTA and location switcher arrive.

Beat 4 · The Room

Candlelit dining room resolves; the site's first cream section slides up and the story begins.

Engineered responsibly: 150vh hero (no dead scroll), frame 1 preloaded for instant paint, mobile-optimized frame counts, and a graded still with slow Ken Burns for prefers-reduced-motion and slow connections.

  • Both locations, one brain. Knows Hell's Kitchen vs East Village hours (including East Village's 2am Fri–Sat close), addresses, cross-streets, and subway directions.

Section 07

Technical Approach

Cinematic on the surface, ruthless engineering underneath.

Single-File Architecture

The entire site ships as one meticulously crafted HTML file with inline CSS/JS — no page-builder bloat, no plugin debt, no Popmenu lock-in. Fast to load, trivial to maintain, impossible to break with a bad plugin update.

GSAP Motion Engine

All animation runs on GSAP + ScrollTrigger — the industry standard behind award-winning sites. Scroll-scrubbed hero frames, pinned location stacks, staggered reveals, and full prefers-reduced-motion fallbacks.

Vercel Hosting + GitHub

Deployed on Vercel's global edge network with the custom domain, automatic SSL, and instant cache invalidation. The GitHub repo auto-deploys on every push — menu price changes go live in under a minute, with full version history and one-click rollback.

Two-Location SEO Schema

Structured data done properly: Restaurant Schema.org markup for each location — distinct NAP, geo, hours (East Village's 2am weekends included), menus, price range, and aggregate ratings — plus Open Graph and Twitter Cards. Satellite domains 301-redirect into buenavistany.com to consolidate authority; the architecture is ready for the Murray Hill location page the day it's announced.

Performance Budget

Frame sequences compressed and lazy-loaded, frame 1 preloaded for instant hero paint, fonts subset and preconnected, images served responsive. Target: sub-2.5s LCP on 4G — cinematic never means slow.

Owned, Not Rented

Buena Vista owns the repo, the domain, the analytics, and every asset we produce — photography prompts, video frames, voice-agent configuration. No monthly template ransom.

Hero Concepts

1

Fuego y Azafrán Selected

A seafood paella over open flame — saffron rice, langoustines, steam and sparks against midnight-navy shadow. Camera pushes in slowly as flames flare. Their flagship dish (and top SEO asset) becomes the opening shot of the brand film.

2

La Barra Dorada

Golden-hour cocktail pour at the bar — smoke, citrus twist, candlelit bottles in amber and navy. The "cocktail destination" story; strong for East Village late nights.

3

Azulejos Vivos

Macro azulejo tiles dissolve into a lavish shared table — ceviche, sangria, tapas. The logo emblem literally comes alive into food. Elegant, but less visceral than fire.
